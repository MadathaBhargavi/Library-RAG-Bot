# Central City Library - Rules, Regulations and Conduct

## Section 1: Opening Hours and General Access

The Central City Library operates according to the following schedule:

- **Regular Weekday Hours**: Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
- **Weekend Hours**: Saturday and Sunday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- **National Holidays**: Closed on all officially recognized state and national holidays.
- **Exam Study Hours**: During university final exam weeks, main reading rooms extend operating hours until 11:30 PM.

All visitors must present a valid Library Card or Student/Faculty ID card at the electronic turnstiles upon entry. Visitors without a card must register at the Security Desk in the main foyer.

## Section 2: Code of Conduct and Noise Policy

To ensure a productive study environment for all patrons, the library enforces three distinct sound zones:

1. **Silent Study Zones (3rd and 4th Floors)**:
   - Absolute silence is required at all times.
   - Mobile phones must be switched to silent mode. No phone calls permitted.
   - Headphone audio must not leak sound.

2. **Quiet Study Zones (1st and 2nd Floors)**:
   - Low-volume whisper conversations permitted for brief consultations.
   - Group collaboration should be kept to a minimum.

3. **Collaborative Learning Commons (Ground Floor)**:
   - Normal conversational speech permitted.
   - Group study tables and media pods available.

### Food and Beverage Policy
- Covered drink containers (water bottles, travel mugs with lids) are permitted in all areas except the Rare Books Room.
- Cold, non-fragrant snacks (nuts, dried fruits, energy bars) are allowed in the Collaborative Commons only.
- Hot food, fast food, and open beverage containers are strictly prohibited throughout the library building.

## Section 3: Library Facilities and Equipment Use

- **Public Computers**: Available on the 2nd Floor. Daily limit of 2 hours per user. Logging in requires student/member credentials.
- **Study Rooms**: 12 private study rooms can be reserved online via the Library Portal up to 7 days in advance for groups of 2 to 8 people. Maximum 3 hours per reservation.
- **Printing and Scanning**: Self-service print stations on every floor. Printing costs $0.10 per page for Black & White and $0.50 per page for Color. Document scanning to email/USB is free of charge.
