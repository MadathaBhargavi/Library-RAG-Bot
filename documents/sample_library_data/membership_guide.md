# Central City Library - Membership and Access Guide

## Section 1: Membership Categories and Eligibility

Central City Library offers membership options tailored to different patron groups:

1. **Student Membership**:
   - Eligible: Enrolled students from partner schools and universities.
   - Cost: Free of charge upon proof of current enrollment and valid photo ID.
   - Validity: 1 Academic Year (Renewable annually).

2. **Faculty and Researcher Membership**:
   - Eligible: Academic staff, researchers, and university faculty.
   - Cost: Free with institutional affiliation letter.
   - Validity: 2 Years.

3. **Community / Public Membership**:
   - Eligible: All local residents aged 16 and above.
   - Annual Fee: $35.00 per year ($20.00 for senior citizens 65+).
   - Requirements: Proof of address (utility bill or driver's license) and photo ID.

4. **Temporary / Visitor Pass**:
   - Eligible: Short-term visitors and visiting scholars.
   - Fee: $10.00 per month for reading room and Wi-Fi access (no physical book borrowing).

## Section 2: Registration and Library Card Issuance

- Registration can be completed online via `https://library.city.gov/register` or in-person at the Circulation Desk (Ground Floor).
- Physical library cards are issued within 15 minutes of verifying identity documents.
- Digital Library Cards are accessible through the Library Mobile App and can be scanned at entry turnstiles and self-checkout kiosks.

## Section 3: Lost Card Replacement and Security

- Lost or stolen library cards must be reported immediately to prevent unauthorized borrowing.
- Card replacement fee is $5.00.
- Patrons remain responsible for all materials checked out on their account prior to reporting a card as lost.
