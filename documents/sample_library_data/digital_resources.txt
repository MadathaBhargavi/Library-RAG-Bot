Central City Library - Digital Resources and IT Services

1. E-Journal and Academic Database Access
The library subscribes to over 45 academic electronic databases. Major subscriptions include:
- JSTOR (Arts, Humanities, Social Sciences)
- IEEE Xplore Digital Library (Engineering, Computer Science, Electronics)
- ScienceDirect / Elsevier (Life Sciences, Medicine, Chemistry)
- ProQuest Dissertations & Theses Global
- Oxford English Dictionary Online and Encyclopedia Britannica

Database Access Methods:
- On-Site Access: Automatically authenticated when connected to the library network.
- Remote / Off-Site Access: Log in via EZProxy using your Library Member ID and Password at https://proxy.library.city.gov.

2. Campus Wi-Fi Network Setup
Network SSID: Library-Guest (Public) & Library-Secure (Members)
Authentication: Select "Library-Secure", enter your Member ID and Account Password.
Encryption: WPA3 Enterprise / AES-256 bit encryption.
Help Desk: IT Support Counter is located on Floor 2, Desk B.

3. E-Book and Audio Book Lending (OverDrive / Libby)
Patrons can borrow digital e-books and audiobooks through the Libby app.
- Borrowing Limit: 5 digital titles at a time.
- Loan Period: 14 days or 21 days (user customizable).
- Automatic Return: Digital books expire automatically; no late fines apply to e-books.
- Hold Limits: Up to 3 active holds allowed simultaneously.

4. Software and Multimedia Workstations
The Multimedia Lab (Floor 2, Room 204) contains 20 High-Performance Workstations equipped with:
- Adobe Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro)
- MATLAB, RStudio, SPSS, and Python Data Science Suite
- AutoCAD and SolidWorks 3D Modeling software
Reservations can be made 24 hours in advance at the IT Service Counter.
